# Zepto Festive Demand Plan — Decision Memo

## Headline
The planned festive demand is **33,656 units**, while committed orders total **33,170 units**, leaving a gap of **486 units**.

## Context
The analysis covers 20 SKUs across four categories. Dry Fruits and Snacks have commitments above plan, while Pooja and Sweets have shortfalls. The baseline uses clean sales weeks to reduce the effect of stockouts.

## Insight
Stockout incidence was **22.50% during Festive weeks**, compared with **6.01% during Regular weeks**. The holdback check found average gaps of -3.37% for Dry Fruits, -2.77% for Pooja, -1.15% for Snacks, and +2.12% for Sweets. Five of the eight focus SKUs need top-up. Ghee 1L has the largest top-up requirement at 777 units, followed by Kaju Katli at 535 units. All eight focus SKUs have an OK shape flag. The cause of category-level differences is unknown.

## Recommendation
- Arrange **777 additional units of Ghee 1L** — Inventory Planner — Before festive allocation.
- Arrange **535 additional units of Kaju Katli** — Category Manager — Before dispatch planning.
- Review the other three at-risk focus SKUs — Supply Planner — Before order finalisation.
- Monitor committed versus planned units — Operations Analyst — Weekly during the festive period.

## Limitations
- Historical sales may not represent future festive demand.
- Lost demand during stockouts is unknown.
- The cause of category-level holdback differences is unknown.
- Coverage probabilities depend on the normal-distribution assumption.
- The focus analysis covers only eight selected SKUs.
